## Description

ClipGrid turns recent clipboard history into a fast, visual grid you can open from anywhere on your Mac.

Press ⌥⌘C, then choose a clip with 1–9 or A–Z. No digging through menus, no account, and no cloud service between you and what you copied.

FAST, KEYBOARD-FIRST ACCESS

• Open ClipGrid globally with ⌥⌘C
• Select any visible clip with 1–9 or A–Z
• Copy a card with one click
• Close instantly with Escape

A CLEAR VIEW OF YOUR HISTORY

• Browse text, links, images, files, and folders
• Filter by content type or source application
• Filter copied files by extension
• Pin important clips above normal history
• Remove one item or clear the complete history
• Pause clipboard monitoring whenever you want

PRIVATE BY DESIGN

ClipGrid works locally on your Mac. It has no account system, cloud synchronization, analytics, advertising SDK, tracking, or developer-operated network service.

Clipboard history is stored in ClipGrid's local Application Support container. Pasteboards marked by their source application as concealed, transient, or auto-generated are skipped on a best-effort basis.

NATIVE FOR macOS

ClipGrid is built with SwiftUI and AppKit. Its global shortcut uses the native macOS hotkey API and does not require Accessibility or Input Monitoring permission.

ClipGrid requires macOS 14 Sonoma or later.
